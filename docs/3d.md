# 3D views

How a Clariti 3D view looks and behaves: the fire-barrier junction, the lifting anchor in its
concrete, the lintel section, a library product's CAD model, and any view a product adds. Every
colour named here is a role in [`viewer.css`](../viewer.css), read after
[`tokens.css`](../tokens.css).

## What the view is for

A 3D view shows the product where it goes, so the engineer can see fit, clearance and evidence.
It is part of the design, not decoration:

- **The product reads first.** It is opaque, in its own material. The construction around it is
  a white card model (below): one matte card, outlined in ink, half see-through where it hides
  the product, or ghosted when the view has a verdict to show.
- **A colour in the scene is a material**, unless it marks evidence (below), and then it glows.
- **The brand accent never paints geometry.** It belongs to the chrome, and only for AI at work.
- **Words come from the host page, not the canvas.** Every state, verdict and caveat is text in
  the page around the view, in the palette's status roles. The canvas draws; it does not explain.

## The white card model

Every view draws the same way: an architectural card model that reads almost like a drawing.

- **One matte card** for the construction: `--model-card` (light `--c-surface`, dark
  `--c-line-strong`), and `--model-shade`, the card a shade down, for a cut or sawn face and for
  a mould under the model (light `--c-sunken`, dark `--c-line`). Matte: roughness about 0.95,
  no metal.
- **Ink outlines** on the edges of every solid of the construction: `--c-ink`, drawn as fat
  lines about 1.6 px wide at 0.85 opacity, so they keep their weight at any distance. A
  product's own model is drawn without them.
- **Steel is matte** `--material-steel`. Rigging, hooks and other hardware drawn as glyphs are
  ink.
- **Half see-through where it hides the product.** Concrete a product is cast into is card at
  0.8 opacity, and the part of the product it hides is drawn through it in ink at 0.3. The
  concrete in front of a cut is a ghost of the card at about 0.12.
- **Zones** painted on the card are tints of `--c-verified-icon` (allowed), `--c-notcov-line`
  (reduced) and `--c-error-icon` (not allowed, hatched), always named in the host page.
- **Dimensions** are thin ink lines at 0.7 opacity with ticks, labelled in the mono face.

## Canvas, light and lens

| Role | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `--viewer-background` | The canvas ground: the page colour, so the model sits in the page. Light `--c-page`, dark the warm near-black. The canvas itself is clear. |
| `--light-sky`, `--light-ground` | The key light (from above and in front, casting soft shadows) in `--light-sky`, and the hemisphere light between the two. |
| `--light-section` | The fill light for a section view. |
| `--viewer-grid-major`, `--viewer-grid-minor` | A floor grid. The white card model stands on a shadow instead. |

- Neutral tone mapping at exposure 1.0, and a soft room reflection at 0.9.
- The model stands on a ground that shows only its shadow (opacity about 0.16).
- A long lens, about 12 degrees, so the model reads like a drawing.

Materials keep their colour in both themes; the card, the ground and the ink change with the
theme, so a model looks the same object in light and dark.

## Materials

**Opaque, for the product and what it is fixed to:**

| Role | Material |
| --- | --- |
| `--material-steel` | Steel: anchors, brackets, channels, lintels, sockets, ties, CAD models |
| `--material-concrete`, `--material-aac`, `--material-brick`, `--material-timber`, `--material-board` | The structure and the facade |
| `--material-barrier`, `--material-coverPlate` | A cavity barrier and the steel plate over it |
| `--material-wool`, `--material-woolLD` | Mineral wool, standard and low density |
| `--material-intum` | An intumescent strip at rest |
| `--material-foam`, `--material-alu`, `--material-tape`, `--material-coating`, `--material-dpc` | Insulation foam, aluminium, tape, coating, damp-proof course |

In a view of a product, the structure and the facade (concrete, blockwork, brick, timber, board,
foam, framing, membranes) are the white card model, at about 0.25 to 0.3 opacity where they
stand in front of the product: the ink outlines carry them, so the card can stay faint. The
structure roles above, and their translucent twins `--material-concreteT`, `--material-aacT`,
`--material-boardT`, `--material-timberT`, `--material-steelT`, `--material-foamT` and
`--material-woolT`, are for a view whose subject is that material. When a view shows a verdict,
it may ghost everything but the product and its fixings to 0.28 or less.

**Drawing aids:** ink outlines (`--c-ink`, above), `--material-edge` for a hairline that must
stay quieter than the outlines (opacity 0.5 to 0.8), `--material-axis` and `--material-marker`
for reference lines and points, `--material-section` for a cut plane.

A product that needs a material Clariti lacks adds a role to `viewer.css` for every view. It
never colours a mesh inline.

## Evidence in the scene

Some views mark what the evidence says about a part of the model. These are the only colours that
glow (an emissive colour, at the strength given):

| Role | Means | Glow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--material-untested` | No tested configuration covers this part. Flagged, not forbidden. | 0.2 |
| `--material-violation` | A genuine breach of a tested configuration. | 0.5 |
| `--material-principle`, `--material-principleWool` | Geometry from established practice, without test evidence. | 0.35, 0.22 |
| `--material-intumHi`, `--material-intumFire` | An intumescent part shown reacting, and the fire side. | as set |

Each one is always paired with words in the host page: a banner or pill in the palette's
warn, assumed or error roles. Colour alone never carries a verdict.

**Evidence basis** (`--basis-*`) says where a piece of evidence comes from: a certificate, an
assessment, a named test, guidance, a standard, a datasheet, an unnamed or unknown source. It
appears on chips and lists, never on geometry.

## Engineering status

Pass, warn and fail live in the chrome around the view, not on the model:

- **Checks pill** (top left): "All checks passed · max 77%" in `--pass`, or "2 of 16 checks
  failing" in `--fail`.
- **Stat strip and docked results** (bottom): the governing utilisation with a dot, `--pass`
  below 70%, `--warn` from 70%, `--fail` when a check fails.

If a view must mark the element a check is about, it uses `--pass`, `--warn` or `--fail` on that
element only, with a label naming the check.

## States

The host page owns every state and its words. The canvas stays hidden until it reports that it
has drawn.

| State | What the host shows |
| --- | --- |
| Loading | A spinner and "Loading 3D view", or the product's own wording ("Loading the lintel…") |
| Ready | The canvas, the toolbar pill and the checks pill |
| No 3D for this product | "No 3D yet" on the library card (`--warn` chip) and a neutral placeholder |
| Geometry unavailable for this size or snapshot | One line on `--viewer-panel`, for example "3D model unavailable for this size" |
| Could not load or draw | One line, for example "Model could not be loaded" |
| Inputs changed since the last run | A veil over the canvas: "Configuration changed" and "Run optimisation to update" |
| Illustrative geometry | The view's title says so, for example "Selected anchor in illustrative concrete" |
| Not tested, or not decided | A banner in `--warn` or the assumed role, with the next step |

## Chrome and interaction

- **Toolbar pill**, bottom centre: reset, then the view presets Top, Front and Iso, then
  Download with the formats the product offers. One pill per view.
- **Orbit** with damping. Pan and zoom where the model is larger than the view.
- **Hover** shows a name chip: `--viewer-foreground` ground, `--viewer-background` text.
- **Selection** has no colour of its own. A view that needs one outlines the part in
  `--viewer-foreground`, never in the accent.
- **Dimensions and labels** are the mono face at 10 px in `--viewer-foreground`.
- **Cut and section views** draw the cut plane in `--material-section` and hatch the cut faces.
- **Theme.** The host sends `{type: "clariti-viewer-theme", theme: "light" | "dark"}` to the view
  (same origin only), and `?theme=` sets the first one. The view recolours in place: camera,
  orbit target, geometry and parameters stay.

## Stills

A library card or a saved design shows a still of the view itself, never a drawing made for it:

- captured from the same view, at about 640 × 400 or 640 × 480, in both themes;
- on `--viewer-background`, so the still matches the view it opens;
- recaptured whenever the palette or the geometry changes.

## Adding a 3D view

1. Read `tokens.css`, then `viewer.css`. Name only these roles.
2. Draw the product opaque in its material, and the construction around it as the white card
   model.
3. Use the evidence roles only for evidence, each paired with words in the host page.
4. Put every state's words in the host page, from the table above.
5. Use the toolbar pill, the theme message and the hover chip as described.
6. Capture stills from the view, in both themes.

## Where views differ today

- The channel and masonry views come from an external renderer and do not read this palette:
  they draw on white in both themes, with their own loading colours.
- `--material-barrier` and `--material-violation` are both reds and sit close together.
- The junction view has no host error state.
- The standalone junction view, opened outside the application, colours its own sliders and
  selected options with the accent. Inside the application those controls are hidden.
